# Oblig

**Vendor and AI governance readiness for fintechs selling into Southeast Asian and Northeast Asian financial institutions.**

Oblig maps your vendor security and AI governance posture directly to the frameworks the banks, insurers, and payment institutions you're selling into actually use to assess you — not generic SOC 2/ISO 27001, though those still matter too.

## Coverage

**Vendor / technology risk:**
- 🇸🇬 Singapore — MAS TRM Guidelines, Notices 658/1121, emerging TPRMG
- 🇲🇾 Malaysia — BNM RMiT, Payment Services Regulatee policy
- 🇮🇩 Indonesia — OJK POJK 1/2026, POJK 11/2022, FSTI governance (POJK 30/2025)
- 🇵🇭 Philippines — BSP Circular 808, Circular 1137, Circular 1105
- 🇰🇭 Cambodia — NBC TCRMG
- 🇯🇵 Japan — FSA Cybersecurity Guidelines (176 response items)
- 🇰🇷 South Korea — EFTA, FSS/FSC oversight
- 🇹🇼 Taiwan — FSC Outsourcing Regulations, Supply Chain Guidelines

**AI governance:**
- 🇸🇬 MAS AI Risk Management Guidelines (in consultation) + FEAT
- 🇲🇾 BNM AI Discussion Paper + AICB industry framework
- 🇮🇩 OJK AI Governance for Indonesian Banking (8-stage lifecycle)
- 🇵🇭 BSP STARS framework (Memorandum M-2026-031)
- 🇰🇭 NBC TCRMG baseline (no AI-specific framework yet — tracked)
- 🇯🇵 FSA AI Discussion Paper series + AI Promotion Act
- 🇰🇷 FSC 7-principle AI guidelines + AI Framework Act (binding, high-impact AI)
- 🇹🇼 FSC Financial AI Guidelines + AI Basic Act (pending)

## Relationship to GRCArc

Oblig is a separate product from [GRCArc](https://github.com/ohkariku-boop/grcarc). GRCArc remains a lightweight, self-serve governance scorecard for early-stage founders getting their first framework in place. Oblig is a deeper, market-specific product for companies whose buyers are regulated financial institutions with real vendor due-diligence requirements.

Different buyer, different urgency, different price point — kept as two products rather than one blended positioning.
